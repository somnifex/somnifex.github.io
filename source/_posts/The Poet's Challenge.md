<p style="text-align: center;">The poet scoffed, "No AI can write a verse so fine,"</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">The painter claimed, "No AI can paint a scene divine."</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">The designer cried, "No AI can craft a work so bold,"</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">The screenwriter swore, "No AI on Hollywood's screen unfolds."</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">... ...</p>

<p style="text-align: center;">But combined, they grasp not what BP means,</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">Nor the dance of probability, unseen.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">How humans learn, a mystery it remains,</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">Yet they judge the AI's potential, it seems.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">... ...</p>

<p style="text-align: center;">The scholars strive, debates they cast aside,</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">To nurture AI, with knowledge as their guide.</p>
